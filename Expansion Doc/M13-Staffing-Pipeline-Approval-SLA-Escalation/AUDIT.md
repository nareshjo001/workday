# M13 Audit

M13 is read-only apart from the PM's existing project-settings update. It introduces no derived-breach write, escalation mutation, or new audit event because response timing is calculated from durable candidate submission and project policy data.

Existing M12 candidate decisions and assignment creation remain transactionally audited. M13 neither changes acceptance authority nor bypasses M08 compliance, M09 relationship access, or M11 availability checks.
