# M20 — Business Dashboards, Financial Health & CSV Exports

M20 presents server-scoped operational dashboards for Vendors, PMs, and Contractors. It deliberately keeps approved work, billable-uninvoiced value, invoice lifecycle totals, settlement totals, outstanding value, and overdue value separate.

Vendor-only margin is based on immutable assignment bill and cost snapshots. PMs never receive cost or margin fields; Contractors receive only their own assignments, timesheets, and document-expiry actions.
