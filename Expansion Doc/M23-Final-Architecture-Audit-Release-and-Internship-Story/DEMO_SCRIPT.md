# 5–8 Minute Portfolio Demo

## 0:00–0:40 — Problem and roles

“This system coordinates contingent workforce work among a Vendor, a client-side PM, and a Contractor. It covers staffing, approved work, billing eligibility, invoicing, settlement, reporting, and safe closeout.” Mention that the first MVP came from a two-day Workday hackathon and this version is the independently expanded portfolio release.

## 0:40–1:30 — PM project and requirement

Sign in as the PM. Show the project, skill requirement, connected Vendor, and staffing pipeline. Point out that the PM owns project decisions.

## 1:30–2:20 — Vendor candidate

Sign in as the Vendor. Show the contractor profile/skill/compliance and submit the contractor to the requirement. Mention that the retired direct-assignment shortcut no longer exists.

## 2:20–2:55 — PM acceptance

Return as PM, open the submitted candidate, and accept. Show the resulting dated assignment. Briefly mention capacity/overlap locks and immutable rate snapshots.

## 2:55–3:40 — Contractor timesheet

Sign in as Contractor, create and submit daily work. Show that draft/submitted/rejected work is distinct from approved work.

## 3:40–4:15 — PM work approval

Approve the timesheet. Show milestone progress and the resulting billable contribution. Say “approved work becomes eligible to invoice; it is not yet invoiced.”

## 4:15–5:15 — Vendor invoice

Build a draft from eligible contributions, show server-derived line items/tax/adjustment/total, then submit. Mention sequential numbering and frozen PDF.

## 5:15–5:50 — PM invoice approval

Review the submitted invoice and approve it. Emphasize: an approved invoice is still unpaid.

## 5:50–6:25 — Payment and dashboards

Record a partial or full payment as Vendor. Show paid and outstanding values, then compare approved work, invoiced, approved invoice, paid, and outstanding on dashboards.

## 6:25–7:00 — Offboarding and engineering close

Show release/project close readiness with blockers/warnings. Close with Docker demo reproducibility, Playwright lifecycle coverage, audit logging, tenant tests, and backup/restore evidence.
